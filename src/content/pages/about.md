title: What this is
---

Sensus Fidei was created with a complicated but necessary goal: to try to redirect
Catholics into living our Faith out in every element of our lives. This is a complicated
task because of the effects that modern culture and principles have had on everyone living
in modernity. It is very easy to underestimate the amount of influence the world can have
on an individual. We tend to think that we have escaped the effects of the culture because
we have our Faith, and because we try to live in accord with our Faith. The reality is that
at every point in human history there are temptations that are relevant to that time and
place, and it can be very difficult to extract those temptations if you have been steeped
in a culture that embraces them.

Some might disagree with this analysis because they participate in counter-cultural ideas
such as the protests against widespread legalization of abortion. It is easy to be a part
of these protests and assume that, because we are fighting this grave evil, we are
completely set apart from the ill effects that have plagued the rest of our society. In a
way this is true and very good; however, we should not use these good acts to excuse the
many other areas of our lives in which we may be falling short of our Catholic duties. My
purpose is not to detract from the current efforts against the evils of our time, but
rather to strengthen those efforts with a reminder that there are many other layers to
living a Catholic life which we have a duty to engage in and constantly perfect ourselves
in.

This then is my purpose: to analyze, as best I can, the ways in which we integrate our
Catholicism into each major duty of a Catholic layman's life and to address the modern
challenges that we face in performing those duties. I am not a theologian, and I lack the
experience many of my readers have, so I welcome and will publish any objections to my
articles.

God bless,\
